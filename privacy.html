Bike Ride Green – Privacy & Safety Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Bike Ride Green (“the App”) is designed with privacy and safety in mind. The App can be used without creating an account. By default, ride data, nutrition entries, weight entries, calorie goals, and saved foods or meals are stored locally on your device.

Bike Ride Green also offers optional online features. If you choose to create an account, you may upload selected ride summaries, view your online dashboard, search the shared food catalogue, participate in private groups, clubs, family groups, or challenges, and use Green Sync to synchronize selected information between compatible Green Portfolio apps.

2. Local Use Without an Account

You can use Bike Ride Green without creating an account. When used without an account, your ride data remains stored locally on your device. The App does not require an account to record rides.

Food entries, manual calorie totals, additional activity entries, calorie-goal history, weight records, and your saved foods or meals remain stored locally on your device. You can export or restore nutrition data using the App’s nutrition backup feature.

3. Optional Online Account

If you choose to create an online account, Bike Ride Green may collect and store the information needed to operate that account, such as:

  • Your email address for account login and authentication.
  • Your optional profile or display name, if provided.
  • Uploaded ride summaries that you choose to send to the online account.
  • Group, club, family, or challenge membership information.
  • Food search requests when you choose to use the optional shared food catalogue.
  • Information synchronized through Green Sync when you enable it, including meals, saved foods, manual daily calorie totals, calorie goals, weight entries, and activity-calorie information.

Creating an account is optional. You control whether you sign in, upload selected ride summaries, participate in online groups, or enable Green Sync.

An online account is required for optional online features, including shared food catalogue searches, uploading selected ride summaries, accessing the online portal, participating in groups or challenges, and using Green Sync. An account is not required for local ride or nutrition tracking.

Using the shared food catalogue search by itself does not upload your personal nutrition entries, weight records, calorie goals, or saved foods or meals. Those items are uploaded only if you separately enable and use Green Sync.

4. Ride Data

Ride data may include information such as ride date, distance, duration, average speed, elevation gain, calories, heart-rate information when available, and personal benchmark values.

By default, ride data is stored locally on your device. Ride summaries are uploaded only when you choose to upload them from the App.

Bike Ride Green is intended to upload ride summaries, not continuously track or sell your personal location data. Export features such as GPX, CSV, or a text activity summary are controlled by you from within the App.

5. Nutrition and Weight Data

The App may allow you to record food, calorie totals, additional activities, calorie goals, weight entries, notes, and saved foods or meals. This information is stored locally on your device by default and is used to provide nutrition summaries, calorie graphs, and weight trends.

If you sign in and enable Green Sync, selected nutrition and activity information—including meals, saved foods, manual daily calorie totals, calorie goals, weight entries, and activity-calorie information—is securely uploaded to your account so it can be synchronized between compatible Green Portfolio apps. Green Sync is optional and can be disabled in the App’s preferences.

Nutrition and weight features are intended for personal information and tracking only. They do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

6. Groups, Clubs, Family Groups, and Challenges

Bike Ride Green may allow users to join private groups, clubs, family groups, or challenges. If you join a group and upload ride summaries, certain ride summary information may be visible within that group dashboard depending on the group settings and features available at the time.

Group dashboards are intended for private sharing, progress tracking, club activity, family activity, or friendly challenges. Do not join a group if you do not want your uploaded ride summary information to be visible to that group.

7. Location Access

The App requires access to your location to record rides and display routes on the map. Location data is used while recording a ride. If you enable background location access, it is used only to record your ride accurately while the App is running in the background.

Location data is not used for advertising or third-party tracking.

8. Third-Party Services and Hosting

Bike Ride Green does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics for user profiling.

If you use the optional online account, account data, uploaded ride summaries, group information, and information you choose to synchronize through Green Sync may be processed and stored by Supabase, the backend service provider used by Bike Ride Green to provide authentication, synchronization, online dashboard, group, club, and challenge features.

When you use the optional shared food catalogue search, your search request may be processed through Bike Ride Green’s secure backend and a food-data provider to return matching food information. Search is used only to provide that requested feature and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Service providers are permitted to process information only to provide their contracted services and are expected to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable privacy requirements.

9. Data Sharing

Bike Ride Green does not sell your personal data.

Your uploaded ride summaries may be shown to you in your online dashboard and may be visible to private groups, clubs, family groups, or challenges that you join or participate in. Bike Ride Green does not share your data with advertisers.

Personal nutrition entries, calorie goals, weight records, and saved foods or meals are not shared with groups, clubs, family groups, advertisers, or challenges. They remain local unless you enable Green Sync, in which case synchronized information is stored privately under your online account for cross-app synchronization.

10. Deleting Online Data and Account Deletion

You may delete your online data from within the App. This removes uploaded online ride summaries, groups, memberships, and profile information from the online account system where applicable.

You may also delete your Bike Ride Green online account from within the App. Account deletion permanently removes your online account, uploaded online ride summaries, group memberships, and portal access. This action cannot be undone.

Deleting your online account does not automatically delete ride data, nutrition data, weight records, or saved foods stored locally on your iPhone. Local data can be removed by deleting entries in the App, clearing history where available, restoring a different backup, or removing the App from your device.

Online information is retained while your account remains active or as needed to provide the online features you choose to use. When you delete your online data or account, associated information is removed from the active online system, except where limited retention is required for security, backup recovery, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

11. Safety Guidelines

While Bike Ride Green helps track your cycling performance, your safety always comes first.

  • Always keep your attention on the road.
  • Do not interact with the App while riding.
  • Use a safe mount if you display live stats.
  • Obey traffic laws and signals.
  • Respect pedestrians and fellow road users.

The App is a tool to assist your cycling experience, but you are responsible for safe riding at all times.

12. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents or guardians should supervise use of the App by minors, especially if optional online account, group, club, or challenge features are used.

13. Changes

This Privacy & Safety Policy may be updated in the future if features change. Any updates will be published at the same URL.
