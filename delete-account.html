Delete your account or online data

Bike Ride Green gives you two permanent deletion options. You can delete synced online data while keeping your account, or delete your complete Bike Ride Green online account and its associated online data.

Delete online data but keep your account

  1. Open Bike Ride Green and go to the main dashboard.
  2. Open the menu and select Online Account.
  3. Sign in, if needed.
  4. Select Delete Online Data, then confirm Delete Data.

This permanently deletes uploaded activities, nutrition information, preferences, weight entries, calorie goals, saved foods, memberships, invitations, and groups associated with your account from Bike Ride Green online services.

Your account remains active. Information stored only on your phone is not deleted.

Delete your complete Bike Ride Green account

  1. Open Bike Ride Green and go to the main dashboard.
  2. Open the menu and select Online Account.
  3. Sign in, if needed.
  4. Select Delete Account, then confirm Delete Account.

This permanently deletes your Bike Ride Green online account and its associated online data. This action cannot be undone.

Information stored only on your phone is not removed automatically. To remove local information, clear Bike Ride Green's app data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Need help? Email support@thegreenportfolio.ca from the address associated with your account. State whether you want to delete only your online data or your complete account. We may need to verify account ownership before completing an email request.

Deletion and retention

Deletion initiated inside the app is processed when the request succeeds. Bike Ride Green does not intentionally retain deleted account content in its active online service. Limited records may be retained only when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or financial recordkeeping, for the period required by applicable law.

For more information about how Bike Ride Green handles data, read the Bike Ride Green Privacy Policy .
